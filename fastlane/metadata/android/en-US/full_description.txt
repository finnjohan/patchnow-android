PatchNow cuts through the flood of new CVEs and shows only the vulnerabilities that need action now: the ones that are actively exploited and critical, on the platforms you run.

<ul>
<li>Combines the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, the NVD (CVSS and CISA's SSVC assessment), EPSS from FIRST and warnings from CERT-SE</li>
<li>"Act now" list: actively exploited, CVSS 9 or higher and reachable over the network without login, plus ransomware-related and CERT-SE warnings</li>
<li>Three levels: Strict, Normal and Broad</li>
<li>Platform filter: Windows Server, Linux, server software and hypervisors by default. Network devices and desktop/mobile can be turned on</li>
<li>Notification when something new becomes critical, with quiet hours at night</li>
<li>Checks in the background every 1, 3 or 6 hours, also when the app is closed</li>
<li>Shows why each vulnerability is critical, CISA's deadline and required action, with links to NVD, CISA and CERT-SE</li>
<li>Swipe right to mark as handled, swipe left to hide a vendor you don't use</li>
<li>Works offline with the last saved data. No account, no tracking</li>
<li>English and Swedish, light, dark or automatic theme</li>
</ul>

The assessment is a help for prioritising, not a guarantee. Always check the vendor's advisory.

This product uses data from the NVD API but is not endorsed or certified by the NVD. PatchNow is not affiliated with CISA, NIST, FIRST or CERT-SE.
